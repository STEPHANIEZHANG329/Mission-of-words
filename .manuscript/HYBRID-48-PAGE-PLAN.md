# 48-Page Consumable Hybrid Plan

Working title: **A Light on Halloween Night: Christian Story & Activity Book for Kids Ages 4-8**

## Pages 1-4 — Front matter / setup
1. Title page
2. Welcome / how to use the book
3. Story setup: Halloween evening, Mia and Eli head to the church fall festival
4. Parent note + Matthew 5:16 reference

## Mission 1 — Shine Your Light (Pages 5-8)
5. Story + coloring hero: Mia and Eli carry lanterns toward church
6. Search & Find: lantern, pumpkin, leaf, Bible, scarf, apple, acorn, basket
7. Maze: help the children reach the church/festival lights
8. Faith action: circle ways to shine light; draw one kind thing you can do

## Mission 2 — God Made the Seasons (Pages 9-12)
9. Story + coloring hero: autumn leaves and changing season
10. Search & Find: sun, moon, star, cloud, leaf, bird, hourglass/season clue
11. Maze: follow the path through the fall trees
12. Faith action: draw your favorite thing God made in autumn

## Mission 3 — A Harvest of Thankfulness (Pages 13-16)
13. Story + coloring hero: apples, corn, bread, pumpkins, family harvest table
14. Search & Find: harvest foods and thank-you objects
15. Maze: carry the harvest basket to the table
16. Faith action: write/draw three things you thank God for

## Mission 4 — Courage Over Fear (Pages 17-20)
17. Story + coloring hero: Mia on the lantern-lit path at the edge of the festival
18. Search & Find: identify ordinary things that can make funny shadows
19. Maze: help Mia follow the safe lit path back to the festival
20. Faith action: circle what you can do when you feel afraid; Isaiah 41:10 reference

## Mission 5 — Share the Harvest (Pages 21-24)
21. Story + coloring hero: apples spill near the harvest table; Mia and Eli help
22. Search & Find: helping tools/objects and harvest items
23. Maze: carry the basket back to the table
24. Faith action: draw or write one way you can help someone this week

## Mission 6 — Kindness at the Fall Festival (Pages 25-28)
25. Story + coloring hero: Mia invites a child standing alone to join the group
26. Search & Find: friendly festival objects / inclusion clues
27. Maze: help the new friend reach the game area
28. Faith action: "Kind hands. Kind words. Room for one more." + Ephesians 4:32 reference

## Mission 7 — God's Beautiful Creation (Pages 29-32)
29. Story + coloring hero: walk home through hills, trees, creek, animals
30. Search & Find: nature objects
31. Maze: follow the creek/path home
32. Faith action: draw one part of creation you are thankful for; Genesis 1:31 reference

## Mission 8 — The Thankful Table (Pages 33-36)
33. Story + coloring hero: family gathers at home
34. Search & Find: table, lantern, pumpkin, food, Bible, leaves, family items
35. Maze: help the children bring a final item to the table
36. Faith action: short prayer / gratitude prompt; 1 Thessalonians 5:18 reference

## Pages 37-44 — Answer keys
37. Mission 1 answers
38. Mission 2 answers
39. Mission 3 answers
40. Mission 4 answers
41. Mission 5 answers
42. Mission 6 answers
43. Mission 7 answers
44. Mission 8 answers

## Pages 45-48 — Consumable back matter
45. Gratitude journal: three writable boxes
46. Prayer walk: write/draw prompts for people, places, and blessings
47. Completion certificate: child writes name/date
48. Closing page: "Keep shining, little light." + small colorable scene

## Consumability check
- Coloring / drawing pages: 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29, 33, 45, 46, 48
- Search & Find / circling pages: 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30, 34
- Maze / pencil-path pages: 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35
- Write / circle / faith-response pages: 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 45, 46, 47

At least 35 of 48 pages require the child to mark, color, write, draw, trace, circle, or solve something. This is materially more consumable than the pure picture-book version.
