## Premise
On Halloween night, two children walk to a church fall festival carrying lanterns. One child feels nervous about the dark and the strange shapes in the evening shadows. Through pumpkins, harvest tables, friendship, creation, prayer, and small acts of kindness, the children discover that darkness does not have to lead the story: God is with them, Jesus calls His people to shine, and kindness can make a dark night feel bright.

## Core promise
This book should feel warm, safe, autumnal, and unmistakably Christian without becoming preachy or frightening. The emotional movement is fear -> reassurance -> kindness -> gratitude -> confident light.
