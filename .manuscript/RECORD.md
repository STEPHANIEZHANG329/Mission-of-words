## Established content
- Title: **A Light on Halloween Night**
- Audience: Christian children ages 4-8 and their families.
- Setting: Halloween evening / church fall festival / home.
- Main emotional arc: mild fear of darkness -> reassurance -> kindness -> gratitude -> confidence.
- Main child characters: Mia and Eli.
- Faith anchors: Matthew 5:16, Isaiah 41:10, Ephesians 4:32, Genesis 1:31, 1 Thessalonians 5:18.
- Recurring symbols: lantern light, pumpkins, autumn leaves, path, harvest table, church/festival lights.

## Reader promises
- The story remains gentle and non-scary.
- Any frightening-looking shadow is resolved into something ordinary and safe.
- Faith is shown through reassurance, service, hospitality, gratitude, and prayer.
- Halloween remains seasonal context rather than occult focus.

## Continuity facts

- Eli encourages Mia and notices opportunities to help.
