## Consumable format
Pages 5-36 contain eight 4-page missions. Each mission uses:
1. Story + coloring hero
2. Search & Find
3. Maze / pencil path
4. Faith action / write-draw page

Pages 37-44 contain answer keys. Pages 45-48 contain writable/drawable back matter and a closing page.

At least 35 of 48 pages require coloring, drawing, writing, tracing, circling, solving, or marking. This is materially more consumable than the read-only picture-book version.
