## Page 3
**A Light on Halloween Night**  
*A Christian Fall Story About Courage, Kindness, and Shining Bright*

## Page 4
For every child who has ever felt a little nervous when the sky gets dark.  
**Matthew 5:16**

## Page 5
Mia and Eli carried little lanterns down the path to the church fall festival.

## Page 6
Pumpkins smiled from porches, leaves skipped across the ground, and lights twinkled ahead.

## Page 7
“It looks different at night,” Mia whispered as the last bit of sunlight slipped away.

## Page 8
Eli held up his lantern. “Then we’ll carry a little light with us.”

## Page 9
The cool wind spun red and gold leaves around their shoes. God had made every season, even this one.

## Page 10
The moon rose over the trees, and the autumn sky stretched wide above them.

## Page 11
A branch scraped softly. *Scritch, scratch.* Mia stopped.

## Page 12
Dad smiled. “You can be brave. God is with you wherever you go.”  
**Isaiah 41:10**

## Page 13
At the festival, baskets overflowed with apples, corn, bread, and bright pumpkins.

## Page 14
Mia breathed in the sweet smell of cider and warm pie.

## Page 15
“Look how much we have to be thankful for,” Mom said.

## Page 16
Mia’s shoulders relaxed. She whispered, “Thank You, God, for good things and good people.”

## Page 17
Later, Mia walked toward a porch glowing at the end of a darker path. Her stomach gave one tiny flip.

## Page 18
A tall shadow stretched beside a tree. For one second, it looked enormous.

## Page 19
Mia lifted her lantern. The shadow became only branches, leaves, and a little fence.

## Page 20
“Dark doesn’t mean I’m alone,” she said. “God is with me.”

## Page 21
Near the harvest table, Eli noticed a family carrying more bags than they could hold.

## Page 22
“We can help,” he said. Together they carried apples and bread to the table.

## Page 23
Then Mia saw a child standing by himself near the festival games.

## Page 24
She remembered that light can look like kindness, too.

## Page 25
“Want to play with us?” Mia asked. The child’s face brightened.

## Page 26
Soon three children were laughing beneath the strings of festival lights.

## Page 27
They played, shared treats, and cheered for one another.

## Page 28
“Be kind to one another,” Mom reminded them.  
**Ephesians 4:32**

## Page 29
On the walk home, the children noticed the hills, trees, clouds, and quiet animals around them.

## Page 30
Even at night, God’s world was full of things to discover.

## Page 31
The creek shimmered. Leaves floated past. A small animal rustled safely in the grass.

## Page 32
“God made all of this,” Eli said. “And He called His creation good.”  
**Genesis 1:31**

## Page 33
Back home, the family gathered around the table with warm food and tired smiles.

## Page 34
They remembered the lanterns, the pumpkins, the new friend, and every little kindness.

## Page 35
Mia folded her hands. “Thank You, God, for staying with me when I was afraid.”

## Page 36
Eli added, “And thank You for giving us light to share.”  
**1 Thessalonians 5:18**

## Page 37
Mia thought about the dark path again. It had not changed, but she had.

## Page 38
She had learned that courage does not mean never feeling afraid. It means remembering who walks with you.

## Page 39
She had learned that light is more than a lantern.

## Page 40
Light can be a helping hand.

## Page 41
Light can be an invitation to someone standing alone.

## Page 42
Light can be gratitude for the world God made.

## Page 43
Light can be a prayer whispered around the table.

## Page 44
And every small light can make a dark night feel a little brighter.

## Page 45
