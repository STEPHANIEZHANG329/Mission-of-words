# Product Pivot — Consumable Christian Halloween Book

## Decision
The 48-page pure picture-book test is not a consumable product. Keep Scriveno for story, continuity, age-fit, and faith QA, but pivot the commercial format to a **story-led consumable activity book**.

## Commercial goal
Create a product a child writes in, colors, solves, traces, circles, and completes so the physical copy is meaningfully used up. This improves repeat-purchase logic compared with a read-only picture book.

## Product concept
**A Light on Halloween Night: Christian Story & Activity Book for Kids Ages 4-8**

Positioning:
- Christian Halloween / fall
- ages 4-8
- black-and-white interior for lower print cost and active use
- story thread carried by Mia and Eli
- activities are part of the story rather than unrelated worksheets
- child-safe, non-occult, non-scary
- parent/church-friendly

## 48-page architecture
- Pages 1-4: front matter / story setup
- Pages 5-36: 8 missions x 4 pages each = 32 consumable activity pages
- Pages 37-44: 8 answer-key pages
- Pages 45-48: prayer, reflection, completion, closing

## Mission template
Each 4-page mission follows one repeated production pattern:
1. **Story + Coloring Hero** — advances the Mia/Eli narrative and gives a large colorable scene.
2. **Search & Find** — child marks/circles items in the environment.
3. **Maze / Path Activity** — child draws through the page.
4. **Faith Action / Write-Draw Page** — child checks, writes, circles, or draws a response.

This makes nearly every interior page physically consumable.

## Eight missions
1. **Shine Your Light** — Matthew 5:16; lanterns, church, pumpkins.
2. **God Made the Seasons** — autumn creation and wonder.
3. **A Harvest of Thankfulness** — gratitude for food, family, and provision.
4. **Courage Over Fear** — dark path becomes ordinary and safe.
5. **Share the Harvest** — helping and serving.
6. **Kindness at the Fall Festival** — welcoming a child who is alone.
7. **God's Beautiful Creation** — nature walk and noticing creation.
8. **The Thankful Table** — home, prayer, family, gratitude.

- editorial QA

## Existing asset advantage
The recovered Bright Hearts assets are much better suited to this format than to a pure picture book because they already contain:
- coloring scenes
- search-and-find pages
- mazes
- faith-response worksheets
- answer keys
- fall/church/harvest motifs

Therefore the earlier visual weakness becomes a product-format advantage after this pivot.

## Economic rule
No new paid image generation until the existing asset set has been fully reused and the 48-page consumable prototype proves that the format works.

## Gate
Proceed to rebuild the current test as a **48-page consumable story + activity prototype**. Do not merge to main and do not authorize paid art yet.
