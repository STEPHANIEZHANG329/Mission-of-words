## Spread rhythm
- Pages 5-12: wonder -> mild fear -> reassurance.
- Pages 13-20: gratitude -> fear challenge -> courage.
- Pages 21-28: service -> inclusion -> kindness.
- Pages 29-36: creation -> gratitude -> prayer.
- Pages 37-44: emotional synthesis and repeated visual motifs.
- Pages 45-48: prayer, family discussion, and closing affirmation.

## Current illustration gap judgment
The recoverable visuals are enough to test narrative flow, but not yet enough for a commercially competitive full-color picture book. Many source previews are black-and-white activity layouts with labels, mazes, or prompts. This branch deliberately tests story structure and image reuse first; it does not claim those previews are final picture-book artwork.
